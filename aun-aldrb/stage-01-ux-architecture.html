01

What this stage fixes, and what it deliberately leaves alone

The site is not architecturally broken. It has a real design-token system, correct RTL logical properties, a working focus trap, and — checked line by line — not one invented claim. What it has instead is drift: the same four trust statements told three times, two conversion sections sitting back to back, and a mobile header that exposes no action at all.

Stage 01 sets the rules that stop the drift. Everything here is structural: order, routing, hierarchy, placement, states. Nothing here changes a colour, a typeface, a photograph, or a single Arabic word.

In scope now

  • The canonical section spine and the question each section answers
  • Journey routing for all four visitor types, including the mobile short-circuit
  • Navigation tiering and the "you are here" contract
  • CTA hierarchy, the four legitimate placement moments, and the ban on the fifth
  • A trust ledger that gives every claim exactly one home
  • Responsive transformation rules — what changes shape rather than merely narrowing
  • The reusable component register and its required states

Deferred by design

  • Stage 02 — header and navigation UX, the first consumer of this spec
  • Stage 03+ — individual section treatments, in spine order
  • Any stage — copy changes, which require explicit instruction that has not been given

03

One conflict in the brief, and how it is resolved

The brief specifies the page order twice, and the two specifications disagree. This has to be settled before anything downstream can be consistent.

Position§03 Information hierarchy§08 Scroll experience
1What we doHero
2Why we are relevantAbout / context
3ServicesWhy us
4Why trust usServices
5How it worksHow we work
6Who we are — about, vision, valuesVision / mission
7ContactValues, then contact

§03 places Services third and pushes من نحن to sixth; §08 places من نحن second and Services fourth. They cannot both be the running order.

Resolution

§08 governs the scroll order. §03 governs the cognitive order, and is satisfied within sections rather than by moving them.

The two documents are describing different things. §03 lists the levels of question a visitor works through; §08 lists the sections they scroll past. One section can answer more than one level — and here, one already does. §03's Level 02, "who we serve plus specialisation plus key trust signals", is answered inside the hero today by the three audience cards (كبار السن, ذوو الإعاقة, المرضى) and the trust strip beneath it. It does not need its own section, and giving it one would push خدماتنا further from the top, not closer.

§08 also happens to match the order already built, which honours the brief's own instruction to preserve existing foundations. Reordering nine sections to satisfy a reading of §03 that §08 contradicts would be churn without a visitor benefit.

04

The spine — nine stops, each answering the last one's question

This is the canonical order. Every later stage designs into it and may not add, remove, or reorder a stop without revising this document. The test for each stop is not "is this section good" but "does it answer the question the previous stop raised, and hand a new one forward".

  1. 01

    الرئيسية — Hero #home

    Answers: What is this, and is it for someone like me? Company, service category, and the three audiences, in that order.

    Hands forward: "Who is behind it?"

  2. 02

    من نحن — About #about

    Answers: Who are you and where do you operate? Saudi company, Riyadh, equipped vehicles, trained staff, support beyond the drive itself.

    Hands forward: "Plenty of firms claim that — why you?"

  3. 03

    لماذا عون الدرب — Why us #why-us

    Answers: What separates you? Six differentiators presented as connected stops on the route, not as a card grid.

    Hands forward: "Fine — what can I actually book?"

  4. 04

    خدماتنا — Services #services

    Answers: Which service matches my situation? Seven named services, scannable by title alone. This is the page's decision point.

    Hands forward: "If I choose one, what actually happens?"

  5. 05

    كيف نعمل — How we work #how-we-work

    Answers: What happens on the day? Three stages — at home, in transit, on arrival. Removes the fear of the unknown that stops a first call.

    Hands forward: "Are you the kind of company I want handling this?"

  6. 06

    رؤيتنا ورسالتنا — Vision & mission #vision-mission

    Answers: What are you trying to be? Direction and purpose, in that order.

    Hands forward: "And how do you behave day to day?"

  7. 07

    قيمنا — Values #values

    Answers: How do you treat the person in your care? Six values. The last reassurance before the decision.

    Hands forward: "I'm convinced. How do I reach you?"

  8. 08

    تواصل معنا — Contact #contact

    Answers: How do I reach you, right now? Phone, WhatsApp, hours, address. Absorbs today's separate #cta block as its opening — see finding P4.

    Hands forward: nothing. This is the terminus, and the only stop with no onward question.

  9. 09

    Footer site-footer

    Answers: Where was that thing I scrolled past? Full information architecture — primary links, secondary sections, contact repeated as reference rather than as a pitch.

The rule this spine enforces

A section earns its place by answering a question the visitor is already holding. If a proposed section answers a question nobody asked at that point in the scroll, it does not belong there — move it or drop it. That single test is what disqualifies today's standalone #cta block, which re-asks a question #contact answers two hundred pixels later.

06

Navigation — two tiers, and a "you are here" that never goes blank

The five-item primary navigation is correct and stays as it is. The secondary sections stay out of the header and remain reachable from the footer. What changes is the relationship between the two tiers.

TierItemsWhere it appears
PrimaryLoad-bearing. Never exceeds five. الرئيسية · من نحن · لماذا عون الدرب · خدماتنا · تواصل معنا Header, mobile drawer, footer quick links — same order in all three
SecondaryReachable, never competing. كيف نعمل · رؤيتنا ورسالتنا · قيمنا Footer only, plus in-page scroll. Never promoted to the header.
ActionNot navigation. Governed by §07. تواصل معنا Header, persistent at every breakpoint

The scroll-spy blanking defect

The current scroll-spy observes every [data-section] and calls setActive(id) with whatever section is centred. When that section is #how-we-work, #vision-mission, #values or #cta, no navigation link matches — so the function strips .is-active and aria-current from every link and sets none. For four of the nine stops, roughly a third of the page by height, the navigation reports the visitor is nowhere at all. Screen-reader users lose aria-current entirely across the same stretch.

Fix — parent mapping, not more nav items

Every secondary section declares the primary item it belongs under, and the spy resolves through it. The header stays at five items; the active state stays continuous.

#how-we-work → #services · #vision-mission → #about · #values → #about · #cta → #contact

Mobile drawer

The drawer's structure is sound — full-screen below the header, focus-trapped, generous tap targets at --fs-h4. Two architectural additions, both serving Journey D:

  • Direct actions above the link list. Call and WhatsApp as their own controls, so a visitor who opens the menu looking for a phone number finds one rather than a link to a section containing one.
  • An active state that is not colour alone. Today .mobile-nav__link.is-active sets colour and nothing else, while the desktop equivalent also draws an underline bar. The drawer needs a non-colour marker to meet the same standard.

One primary action exists on this site: تواصل معنا. Everything else is a means of performing it or a way of learning more before doing so.

The four legitimate moments

The brief lists five placements. On the §08 running order, two of them — "after service understanding" and "after the trust and value proposition" — land within a screen of each other, because Why-us precedes Services here. They collapse into one. Four moments, not five:

#MomentRendered asCurrent state
1Header, persistentPrimary buttonHidden <768px
2Hero, after the value propositionPrimary + tertiary pairCorrect
3End of Services — the decision pointPrimary, inside the grid as its closing tileCorrect
4Contact — the terminusSecondary pair: WhatsApp and callPreceded by a duplicate

Where the current build overspends

The string تواصل معنا appears as an interactive control eight times on one page: header, hero, Services tile, the #cta block, the drawer button, and as a navigation item in the header, drawer and footer. Two of those — the Services tile and the #cta block — fire barely a screen and a half apart, and #cta then hands directly to #contact, which asks a third time.

Two rules that resolve it

Adjacency ban. Two consecutive stops may not both carry a primary CTA. A section that only introduces the next one is not a stop — it is that section's opening.

No backward tertiaries at the terminus. #cta currently offers اكتشف خدماتنا as its secondary action, sending a visitor who has read the entire page back up to stop 04. At the moment of conversion the only available directions are "act" and "keep reading downward".

What this means for the hero

The hero's four-item trust strip restates what Why-us says in full one screen later. It is the single largest block of pure repetition on the page and, on a phone, roughly 300 px of it. The hero keeps the audience cards, which nothing else carries; the trust strip's job belongs to Why-us, which already does it better with six items instead of four.

This is a placement decision, not a copy decision. No approved wording is altered or deleted — the claims continue to appear, in the section that owns them.

09

Responsive architecture — three tiers, and what transforms

The existing breakpoints stay: 480 / 768 / 1024 / 1280, with the twelve-column grid dropping to four below 768. What this stage adds is the rule about what changes at each tier. Narrowing a desktop layout is not designing for mobile.

TierCompositionWhat must transform, not merely narrow
Desktop≥1024px 12 columns, --container-wide 1360px Reference composition. Sticky sub-columns permitted where a section has a stable anchor, as Why-us already does.
Tablet768–1023px 12 columns, reduced spans Complexity drops, hierarchy does not. Three-up grids go two-up. Decorative route graphics reduce in opacity before anything textual is touched.
Mobile≤767px 4 columns, single reading column Hero sheds supporting blocks rather than stacking them. Header keeps a visible action. Decorative graphics are removed entirely — the hero road already does this correctly.

The mobile hero is roughly two full screens

Measured against a 390×844 viewport, the current hero stacks: eyebrow, headline, lead, divider, three audience cards at full width, two full-width buttons, a 4:3 image, and a four-row trust strip. That totals approximately 1,550 px — about two complete screens before من نحن begins. §10 asks for no overly tall hero; §11 asks that decoration be reduced before readability. Both point the same way.

Mobile priority order, binding on every stage

  1. What is عون الدرب — headline and lead
  2. What it provides — the service category
  3. Who it is for — the three audience cards
  4. Why trust it — deferred to Why-us, never duplicated in the hero
  5. What I can do now — an action reachable without scrolling

Section transitions

Continuity comes from alternating grounds — --surface, --bg, --bg-tinted, --surface-inverse — not from borders. The current sequence has one flaw: #cta is navy, #contact is light, and the footer is navy again. A light strip sandwiched between two dark blocks reads as an interruption exactly where the page should feel most continuous. Merging #cta into #contact resolves the rhythm and the CTA duplication in one move.

The route graphic is a connective device, used only where it carries meaning — journey, progression, arrival. It appears in the hero, in Why-us as the line joining six stops, in How-we-work as three stages, and in Vision→Mission as the link between direction and purpose. That is the whole permitted list. It does not become a background texture.

11

Accessibility contract

Binding on every stage. The current build already satisfies most of it; two items below are open defects.

  • Focus is always visible. Including on navy grounds, where the blue ring measures 1.84:1 and is replaced with white at 8.53:1 — already handled and to be preserved.
  • Never colour alone. Active, selected, and error states all carry a second signal. Open The mobile drawer's active link is colour-only.
  • Primary blue is a fill colour, not a text colour on light grounds. Open #4975BA on the page ground #F6F8FB measures 4.35:1 — below the 4.5:1 threshold. It affects .section-label__num at 15px semibold in #about, #services and #contact. The fix is a token, not a brand change: primary-coloured text resolves to navy #2C4C82 at 8.53:1, which is already in the palette. Blue keeps every fill, icon and graphic use.
  • 44px minimum targets on every interactive element, met today via --tap-min.
  • Semantic structure and RTL order — one h1, ordered headings, <ol> where sequence is real, logical properties throughout.
  • Motion never carries meaning and always yields to prefers-reduced-motion.
12

Motion and performance budget

Interactions stay in the existing token range: --dur-fast 150ms for feedback, --dur-normal 260ms for transitions, --dur-slow 420ms for entrances. No parallax, no scroll-linked animation, no hover effect that moves layout.

The reveal system is a hard dependency on JavaScript

[data-reveal] sets opacity:0 in CSS and relies on script to add .is-inview. There is no <noscript> anywhere in the document. If the script fails to parse, is blocked, or errors before that block runs, the page renders as an empty shell — headline, lead, every card and every section invisible, on a site whose audience includes people using assistive technology and older devices.

The reveal also delays the largest contentful paint on purpose: above-fold elements are staggered at 70ms intervals and then transition over 420ms, so the headline finishes painting several hundred milliseconds after it could have.

Fix — invert the default

Content is visible unless script says otherwise. An inline script in <head> marks the document as script-capable; the opacity:0 rule applies only under that mark. Nothing is hidden by a stylesheet that a failure can strand.

Above-fold elements are exempt from the stagger entirely — the hero headline and lead paint immediately, and the reveal applies from the second stop onward.

Two further constraints carried forward: every media frame reserves its box with aspect-ratio before load, and the hero's LCP image — currently a 1200×1200 asset served from a third-party host with no srcset and no preconnect, down to a 350 px-wide box on a phone — is self-hosted and responsive before launch. The source file's own comment already flags this.

13

Findings, ranked

Ten defects in the current build, ordered by how much visitor value each one costs. P1–P3 are architectural and belong to this stage's remit; P4–P10 are scheduled against the stage that owns the affected surface.

P1

The mobile header exposes no action

Stage 02
Evidence
.site-nav,.site-header__cta{display:none} at max-width:767px. The hamburger is the only interactive control in the mobile header.
Impact
Journey D — the visitor with the most urgent need — must open a menu before they can do anything. Three taps and a jump past seven sections to reach a phone call. The phone number itself is not visible anywhere until stop 08.
Fix
A persistent action beside the menu trigger at every breakpoint, icon-only if space demands, with an accessible label. The drawer keeps its own CTA and gains direct call and WhatsApp controls above the link list.
P2

The page is invisible without JavaScript

Stage 02
Evidence
[data-reveal]{opacity:0} in CSS; .is-inview added only by script; zero <noscript> in 2,058 lines. 40+ elements carry the attribute, including the h1.
Impact
Any script failure renders a blank page rather than a degraded one. Also delays LCP by the stagger plus a 420ms transition.
Fix
Gate the hiding rule behind a script-set class on the root element, and exempt above-fold content from the stagger.
P3

The mobile hero runs about two full screens

Stage 04
Evidence
At ≤767px the audience cards go single-column, the media frame becomes 4:3, buttons go full width below 480px, and the trust strip stacks to four rows below 520px. Estimated total ≈ 1,550px at 390×844.
Impact
Two screens of hero before من نحن. Inverts the §11 mobile priority order — supporting material outweighs the message.
Fix
Hero on mobile carries headline, lead, audience cards, action pair, image. The trust strip returns to Why-us, which owns those claims per §08's ledger.
P4

#cta and #contact are adjacent duplicates

Stage 11/12
Evidence
Two consecutive sections, both headed تواصل معنا in intent, one directly above the other. Grounds run navy → light → navy into the footer.
Impact
Asks the same question twice at the moment of decision, and interrupts the closing rhythm with a light band between two dark ones. #cta's secondary action also points backward to stop 04.
Fix
Fold #cta into #contact as its opening block. Drop the backward tertiary. Nine stops become eight sections plus the footer.
P5

Scroll-spy blanks the navigation across a third of the page

Stage 02
Evidence
setActive(id) matches links by href. Four observed sections have no corresponding link, so all links lose .is-active and aria-current.
Impact
No "you are here" for four of nine stops, and no aria-current for screen-reader users across the same stretch.
Fix
Parent mapping, per §06. Header stays at five items.
P6

Primary blue fails AA as text on the page ground

Stage 03
Evidence
#4975BA on #F6F8FB = 4.35:1, below 4.5:1. Affects .section-label__num at 15px semibold in three sections. On pure white it is 4.63:1 — passing, but with no margin.
Impact
Section numbering, a wayfinding element, sits under the AA threshold on three of nine stops.
Fix
Split the role at token level: navy #2C4C82 (8.53:1) for primary-coloured text on light grounds, blue for every fill and graphic. Both are already brand colours — the identity does not change.
P7

Four trust claims are told three times before Services

Stage 04/06
Evidence
Hero trust strip, About facts, and Why-us items 01–04 restate the same four claims in near-identical wording. See the ledger in §08.
Impact
Roughly 300px of pure repetition on a phone, and a page that reads as padded rather than confident.
Fix
One claim, one home. Placement only — no approved wording is altered.
P8

Mobile drawer active state is colour-only

Stage 02
Evidence
.mobile-nav__link.is-active{color:var(--color-primary)} and nothing further. The desktop link adds an ::after underline bar.
Impact
Direct §12 violation on the breakpoint where the drawer is the only navigation.
Fix
Add a non-colour marker matching the desktop treatment.
P9

Hero LCP image is third-party, oversized, and unresponsive

Pre-launch
Evidence
fetchpriority="high" on a 1200×1200 asset from i.ibb.co, no srcset, no preconnect for that host, rendered into a ~350px box on a phone.
Impact
An extra DNS and TLS handshake on the critical path, and several times the necessary bytes on mobile.
Fix
Self-host, add a <picture> with srcset. The source file's own comment already calls for this.
P10

Off-system gold hardcoded in the hero

Stage 03 — decision needed
Evidence
#8a6a12 and #b08a2e written directly into .hero__eyebrow-gold and .hero__divider. Neither exists in the token set, whose own header states that sections must never hard-code values.
Impact
An accent outside the approved palette, invisible to any future brand change because it bypasses the tokens.
Fix
Not a Stage 01 call. Either promote it to a named brand token or remove it — a decision for whoever owns the identity, flagged here so it is made deliberately rather than inherited by default.

15

Handoff to Stage 02

Stage 02 covers the global header and navigation UX only. It inherits these decisions and resolves four of the ten findings. It does not touch any section body.

  1. Restore a persistent header action at every breakpoint — resolves P1, and with it test questions 3, 4 and half of 9.
  2. Add parent mapping to the scroll-spy so the active state never blanks — resolves P5 and question 8.
  3. Give the drawer a non-colour active marker and direct call/WhatsApp controls above the link list — resolves P8 and completes Journey D.
  4. Gate the reveal system behind a script-set root class — resolves P2. It sits in the global layer, so it belongs to the header stage rather than to any section.

Unchanged and not open for revision in Stage 02: the five primary navigation items and their order, the two-tier navigation model, the sticky header behaviour, the token system, the brand palette, and every word of approved Arabic copy.
