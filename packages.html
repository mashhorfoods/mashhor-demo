Good to know

Basic includes 2 rounds, Standard 3 rounds, and Premium 4 rounds. A round is counted once you send consolidated feedback, so it’s worth gathering notes before each pass.

50% on project approval and kickoff, and 50% on completion, before final delivery. Prices are fixed for the scope shown in each package.

The project timeline starts once we have your content, the required materials, and the kickoff payment. Late content or feedback during the project may shift the delivery date.

Final files are delivered as JPG, PNG and PDF (Premium also includes website design files). Editable source files aren’t delivered automatically, but can be added as an add-on if you need them.

Development, hosting, domains, copywriting and SEO sit outside the packages. Anything beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately as an add-on, and final files are handed over once due payments are complete.
